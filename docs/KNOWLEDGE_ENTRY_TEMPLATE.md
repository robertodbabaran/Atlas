# Knowledge entry template

Every knowledge-base entry follows this shape. An entry without a source that someone opened is marked unverified and is not published as fact.

    Title: one plain statement, for example "Sodium has an adequate intake and an upper limit"
    Claim: one or two sentences
    Evidence grade: A trial evidence, B large observational, C weak or expert opinion, D assumption
    Source: author, year, title, link
    Opened by a person: yes or no, and the date
    Numbers: each figure with its unit and the population it applies to
    Caveats: who it does not apply to, and what the source could not show
    Last checked: date

## Rules

- State what the source shows, not more. Say "associated with" for observational evidence.
- Give the range or interval when the source gives one.
- Do not copy long passages. Summarise and link.
- If two sources disagree, record both.
- This knowledge base is general information and not medical advice.
