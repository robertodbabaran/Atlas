# Disclaimer

## Not medical or nutrition advice

Atlas is a bookkeeping and visualization tool. It records what you enter and compares it with targets you set. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and nothing in this repository is medical, dietary or nutritional advice.

Talk to a qualified clinician before you change your diet or training, especially if you take medication or have a medical condition. Some foods and supplements interact with medication.

## Accuracy

- Figures depend on what you enter and on the nutrient data you load. Photo-based and estimated entries can be off by a wide margin. Atlas labels every figure as label, weighed, estimate or unconfirmed, and you should read the label.
- Knowledge-base notes are general information. Each one cites its source. A note without a verified citation is marked as unverified and should not be relied on.
- Sample data in this repository is invented. It does not describe any real person.
